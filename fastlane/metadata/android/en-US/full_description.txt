Seamless is an offline video player built around two ideas: short videos deserve an immersive, swipeable feed, and everything else deserves to open fast, in a player that is a pleasure to look at.

<b>Why Seamless</b>

• <b>Every short video on your phone, in one feed.</b> Seamless gathers the short clips from across the whole device and plays them full screen, one swipe apart. The next clip is already decoded and waiting, so there is no black frame and no spinner between them.
• <b>Made to be looked at.</b> Liquid glass, frosted glass or an accent island for the navigation bar. Six timelines, from a live waveform to a film strip. Dark or light, in the accent colour you choose.
• <b>Fast, whatever you open.</b> Films and landscape clips go straight into playback, and a video handed over by your gallery or file manager opens in the same player.
• <b>Subtitles, found for you.</b> Tap Find subtitles and Seamless looks the video up on OpenSubtitles. An exact match can be applied without asking, and it is kept, so it plays offline from then on.

<b>The feed</b>

• Swipe between clips with no perceptible gap
• True shuffle: nothing repeats until everything has played
• The whole device, or only the folders you choose
• A wall of clips to pick from, as masonry, grid or list
• Double tap to skip or to mark a favourite, hold for double speed, share any clip

<b>The player</b>

• Floating controls, with six timeline styles and buttons to match
• Drag for brightness and volume, with volume past 100% when you need it
• Double tap to jump ten seconds, hold for 2x
• Subtitles inside the file or beside it, sized and placed the way you like
• Resume where you left off, lock the screen, drag down to close
• Fit, crop or stretch

<b>The library</b>

• Grid or list, with search, and an order of its own for every folder
• Rename, move, delete, pin and share
• Hide folders, or lock them behind your fingerprint, face or PIN
• Export your settings to a file, or to Google Drive through the system picker, and bring them back

<b>Privacy</b>

No ads, no analytics, no accounts, no tracking. Seamless uses the internet for one thing only: looking up a subtitle when you ask it to. That is off until you switch it on and add your own free OpenSubtitles key, and your videos are never uploaded.
