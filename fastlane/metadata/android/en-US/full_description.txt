Seamless turns a folder of short vertical videos into a continuous, swipeable feed — and
still plays ordinary long-form video properly.

<b>Why it exists</b>

Offline short-form video is badly served. Fast players tend to open only one file at a
time; folder players tend to show a black frame between every clip.

That gap is not a tuning problem. A conventional player owns a single decoder and, for
every file, tears it down and sets it up again. That costs a fraction of a second even
from local storage, and no amount of cache tweaking removes it.

Seamless keeps several players alive at once and prepares the neighbouring clips while the
current one is still playing, so swiping simply changes which one you see.

<b>The feed</b>

* Swipe up and down between clips with no perceptible gap
* True shuffle — nothing repeats until everything has played, and the order differs next time
* Play from folders you choose, or scan the whole device and filter by shape and length
* Brightness and volume by dragging sideways, double tap to skip, hold for double speed

<b>The player</b>

* Clean floating controls, with elapsed and remaining time
* Portrait clips roll into the next one; landscape and long videos return to the list
* Fit, crop or stretch
* Resume where you left off, for long videos only
* Screen lock, so a phone in a pocket cannot skip ahead
* Drag down from the top to close
* Opening a video from another app returns you to that app

<b>The library</b>

* Grid or list, with search and sorting
* Rename, move, delete and share
* Hide folders you would rather not see
* Lock folders behind your fingerprint, face or device PIN — a locked folder shows no
  preview and is kept out of the feed

<b>Privacy</b>

Seamless has no internet permission at all. It cannot send anything anywhere, so there is
no analytics, no advertising, no accounts and no tracking. It reads your videos for the
only reason a video player would: to list them and play them.
